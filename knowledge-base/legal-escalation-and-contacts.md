## Urgent matters

Call the legal on-call number (extension 4400) for:

- A legal notice, court summons or police inquiry received at any site
- A suspected personal data breach
- A regulator arriving on site
- A threat of legal action from a customer about a live shipment

Outside business hours, on-call rotates weekly between commercial and disputes counsel.

## Escalation path

1. The area contact above
2. If no answer within 1 business day, or the matter is urgent: senior counsel
3. If unresolved, or it needs a decision above senior counsel: General Counsel's office
