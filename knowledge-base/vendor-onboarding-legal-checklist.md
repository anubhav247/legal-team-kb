# Vendor Onboarding Legal Checklist

Legal steps before procurement can activate a new vendor. Procurement owns the overall
onboarding; legal owns the items below.

## Checklist

| # | Item | Required when | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sanctions and adverse-media screening | Always | Screening report attached to the vendor record |
| 2 | Signed NDA | Before any confidential discussion | Executed [Mutual NDA](nda-standard-terms.md) |
| 3 | Signed MSA or purchase order terms | Before work starts | Executed agreement in the contract repository |
| 4 | Data processing agreement | Vendor touches personal data | Executed [DPA](data-processing-agreement.md) |
| 5 | Anti-bribery declaration | Annual value over INR 10 lakh | Signed declaration form LEG-F-022 |
| 6 | Insurance certificates | On-site work or logistics services | Public liability and goods-in-transit cover, valid dates |
| 7 | Conflict-of-interest check | Always | Requester's declaration in the intake form |

## Red flags that stop onboarding

- The vendor refuses any audit right
- The vendor is owned by a current or recent Meridian employee and this was not declared
- Bank account country differs from the vendor's registered country, without explanation
- Screening returns a sanctions match, even a possible one

Report red flags to the compliance contact in [Legal Escalation and Contacts](legal-escalation-and-contacts.md).
Do not tell the vendor why onboarding stopped.

## Re-checks

Screening (item 1) repeats every 12 months for active vendors. Insurance certificates are
checked again on their expiry date.
