# Mutual NDA Standard Terms

The key terms of Meridian's mutual non-disclosure agreement (template LEG-T-001, version 4.2).
Use it unchanged for self-serve signature. Any edit sends it to legal review.

## Key terms

| Term | Standard position |
|---|---|
| Type | Mutual: both parties disclose and both protect |
| Definition of confidential information | Anything disclosed in any form, marked or not, that a reasonable person would see as confidential |
| Purpose | Evaluating a possible business relationship, named in Schedule 1 |
| Term of agreement | 2 years from signature |
| Confidentiality survives | 3 years after the agreement ends; trade secrets for as long as they stay trade secrets |
| Return or destruction | Within 30 days of a written request; one archival copy allowed for legal compliance |
| Governing law | Laws of India |
| Jurisdiction | Courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka |
| Remedies | Injunctive relief available without proving damages |

## Standard exclusions

Information is not confidential if the receiving party can show it was:

1. Already public, or became public without breach by the receiving party
2. Already known to the receiving party without a duty of confidence
3. Received from a third party lawfully, without a duty of confidence
4. Developed independently without using the disclosed information

## Common requests and answers

| Counterparty asks for | Answer |
|---|---|
| One-way NDA in their favour | Acceptable if we disclose nothing. Legal review, Tier 1. |
| 5-year term | Acceptable for survival, not for the agreement term |
| Their governing law (outside India) | Escalate to commercial counsel |
| Non-solicitation clause | Not in an NDA. Refuse and refer to the [Contract Clause Library](contract-clause-library.md). |
