# Data Processing Agreement Standard

Every vendor that handles personal data for Meridian must sign our data processing
agreement (DPA, template LEG-T-014) before any data is shared. This covers the Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and, for EU customer data, the GDPR.

## When a DPA is required

| Situation | DPA needed? |
|---|---|
| Vendor stores or accesses employee, customer or driver personal data | Yes |
| Vendor sees only business contact details for account management | No, the MSA confidentiality clause is enough |
| Vendor processes data outside India | Yes, plus a transfer impact note |
| Sub-processor of an existing vendor | Covered by the vendor's DPA, if listed in its sub-processor schedule |

## Required terms

1. Processing only on Meridian's documented instructions
2. Personal data breach notice to Meridian within **24 hours** of discovery
3. Security measures listed in Annex 2, at least ISO 27001 aligned
4. Sub-processors: prior written notice, 30 days to object
5. Assistance with data principal requests within 5 business days
6. Deletion or return of all personal data within 30 days of contract end, with written certification
7. Audit right once a year, or after any breach

## Who reviews

All DPAs are Tier 3 under the [Contract Review Process](contract-review-process.md). The
privacy counsel listed in [Legal Escalation and Contacts](legal-escalation-and-contacts.md)
approves any deviation from the required terms.

## Breach handling

If a vendor reports a breach, forward it to the privacy inbox at once. Do not reply to the
vendor about liability; privacy counsel leads all communication.
