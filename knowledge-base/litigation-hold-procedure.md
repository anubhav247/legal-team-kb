# Litigation Hold Procedure

What happens when Meridian expects a dispute, and what every employee who receives a hold
notice must do.

## When a hold starts

Disputes counsel issues a litigation hold as soon as a dispute is reasonably expected, for
example on receiving a legal notice, a regulator's inquiry, or a credible threat of a claim.
Waiting for a court filing is too late.

## What the hold covers

All documents and data that could be relevant, in any form: email, chat messages, shared
drives, laptops, phones used for work, paper files, and records in business systems such as
the transport management system.

## If you receive a hold notice

1. Acknowledge it within **2 business days** using the link in the notice.
2. Do not delete, edit, or move anything covered, even if a retention policy would normally delete it.
3. Do not create new documents commenting on the dispute.
4. Tell disputes counsel about anyone else who holds relevant information.
5. Keep the hold notice confidential.

## IT actions

| Action | Owner | Within |
|---|---|---|
| Suspend automatic deletion for named custodians | IT operations | 1 business day |
| Preserve mailboxes and chat history | IT operations | 1 business day |
| Image laptops of departing custodians before reissue | IT service desk | Before reissue |

## Release

Only disputes counsel can release a hold, in writing, after the matter and any appeal period
end. Normal retention rules then apply again.

Questions go to the disputes contact in [Legal Escalation and Contacts](legal-escalation-and-contacts.md).
