# Contract Clause Library

Approved wording for clauses that come up in most negotiations. Copy the clause as written;
changes need commercial counsel.

## Governing law and disputes

> This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute shall be referred to
> arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, by a sole arbitrator.
> The seat of arbitration is Bengaluru, and the language is English.

Use for all domestic contracts. For foreign counterparties, escalate before agreeing to a
foreign seat.

## Force majeure

> Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable
> control, including natural disasters, war, epidemic, or government action, provided it
> notifies the other party within 7 days and uses reasonable efforts to resume. If the event
> lasts more than 60 days, either party may terminate on written notice.

Payment obligations are never excused by force majeure.

## Non-solicitation

> During the term and for 12 months after, neither party shall solicit for employment any
> employee of the other party who was directly involved in the services. General
> advertisements are not solicitation.

Belongs in an MSA, never in an NDA (see [Mutual NDA Standard Terms](nda-standard-terms.md)).

## Assignment

> Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, which
> shall not be unreasonably withheld, except to an affiliate or a successor in a merger.

## Notices

> Notices must be in writing and sent by email with confirmation, or by courier, to the
> addresses in Schedule 1. Legal notices to Meridian must copy legal-notices@meridianfreight.example.
