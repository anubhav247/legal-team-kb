# Contract Review Process

How a contract moves from a business request to a signed agreement at Meridian Freight Systems.

## Intake

Submit every contract through the Legal Intake form, never by email to a lawyer directly.
Attach the counterparty's draft in an editable format (`.docx`), the commercial summary,
and the expected annual value. Requests without an annual value are returned.

## Review tiers and turnaround

| Tier | When | Reviewer | Target turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 0 | Our NDA template, unchanged | Self-serve, no legal review | Same day |
| Tier 1 | Our template with edits, or value under INR 25 lakh | Legal operations analyst | 3 business days |
| Tier 2 | Counterparty paper, or value INR 25 lakh to 5 crore | Commercial counsel | 7 business days |
| Tier 3 | Value over INR 5 crore, uncapped liability, or personal data processing | Senior counsel plus General Counsel sign-off | 15 business days |

Turnaround starts when intake is complete, not when the request arrives.

## Redlines

- Use tracked changes only. Clean versions from the counterparty are compared against our last version before review.
- Every deviation from the [Master Services Agreement Negotiation Playbook](master-services-agreement-playbook.md) needs a comment explaining why.
- Fallback positions beyond the playbook need the approval of the counsel named in the [Legal Escalation and Contacts](legal-escalation-and-contacts.md) page.

## Approval and signature

After legal approval, the business owner gets sign-off from the people listed in the
[Signature Authority Matrix](signature-authority-matrix.md). Legal operations sends the
final version for e-signature and files the executed copy in the contract repository
within 2 business days.

## Renewals

Contracts with auto-renewal are flagged 90 days before the notice deadline. The business
owner decides to renew, renegotiate or terminate; silence counts as renew.
