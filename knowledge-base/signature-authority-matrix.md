# Signature Authority Matrix

Who may sign contracts on behalf of Meridian Freight Systems Pvt Ltd. Signing outside
this matrix makes the signer personally accountable and the contract may be voidable.

## Limits by total contract value

Total contract value means the full value over the initial term, including taxes,
not the annual value.

| Total contract value (INR) | Business sign-off | Finance sign-off | Signatory |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 10 lakh | Department head | Not required | Department head |
| 10 lakh to 1 crore | Vice president | Finance controller | Vice president |
| 1 crore to 10 crore | Chief Operating Officer | Chief Financial Officer | Chief Operating Officer |
| Over 10 crore | Chief Executive Officer | Chief Financial Officer | Chief Executive Officer, plus board resolution |

## Always need General Counsel approval, whatever the value

- Uncapped liability or an indemnity without a cap
- Exclusivity or non-compete obligations on Meridian
- Transfer of Meridian intellectual property (see [Intellectual Property and Open Source Policy](ip-assignment-and-open-source-policy.md))
- Any agreement with a government body or public sector undertaking
- Settlement agreements and litigation-related documents

## Delegation

Signatories may delegate in writing for up to 30 days, to a named person one level below,
using the Delegation of Authority form. Delegations are logged by legal operations. A
delegate cannot delegate again.

## NDAs

Department heads may sign our [Mutual NDA Standard Terms](nda-standard-terms.md) unchanged
at any level. An NDA with changes follows Tier 1 of the [Contract Review Process](contract-review-process.md).
