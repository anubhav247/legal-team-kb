# Master Services Agreement Negotiation Playbook

Standard positions and approved fallbacks when Meridian buys or sells services under a
master services agreement (MSA). Positions beyond the last fallback need counsel approval.

## Limitation of liability

| Position | Text summary | Who can agree |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Each party's total liability capped at fees paid in the 12 months before the claim | Legal operations analyst |
| Fallback 1 | Cap at 1.5 times the 12-month fees | Commercial counsel |
| Fallback 2 | Cap at 2 times the 12-month fees, with a separate super-cap of 3 times for data breaches | Senior counsel |
| Never | Uncapped liability, except for the carve-outs below | General Counsel only |

Carve-outs from the cap, always: fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury caused
by negligence, and breach of confidentiality.

## Indemnities

- Standard: mutual indemnity for third-party claims of IP infringement and for breach of law.
- Fallback: accept a supplier-only IP indemnity when we are the customer.
- Never accept an indemnity for "any loss arising from the agreement".

## Payment terms

| We are | Standard | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | 60 days from receipt of a valid invoice | 45 days |
| Supplier | 30 days from invoice date | 45 days, with 1.5% monthly late interest |

## Termination

- For convenience: either party, 90 days' written notice. Fallback: 60 days.
- For breach: 30 days to cure after written notice.
- On termination, the supplier hands back all data under the [Data Processing Agreement Standard](data-processing-agreement.md).
